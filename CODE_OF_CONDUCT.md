# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Contributors must communicate respectfully, focus criticism on technical work,
and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or publication of private
information. Good-faith disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the Recode project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban participants when
behavior is unsafe or persistently disruptive.
